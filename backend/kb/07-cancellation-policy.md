# Cancellation and Closure Policy

## Customer-initiated cancellation

A customer may request account closure at any time. The employee must
confirm no pending transactions or holds exist, disclose any applicable
early-closure fee (see fee schedule), and provide a written confirmation
of the closure request within one business day.

## Retention conversations

It is acceptable to ask an open-ended question about the customer's reason
for leaving and to mention relevant account features they may not be aware
of. It is not acceptable to delay or make closure difficult, condition
closure on a retention offer, or imply that closure is not possible.

## Data retention after closure

Standard record-retention policy applies after closure; the employee
should tell the customer their records are retained per regulatory
requirements and are not immediately deleted.
