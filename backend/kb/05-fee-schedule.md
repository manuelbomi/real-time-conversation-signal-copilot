# Standard Fee Schedule (Reference)

## Account maintenance

Standard tier accounts carry no monthly maintenance fee if the average
balance stays above the published minimum for the account type. Below-
minimum accounts are charged the published low-balance fee.

## Transaction fees

Standard transactions are included in the base tier. Expedited or manual-
assist transactions carry the published expedited-service fee, which must
be disclosed to the customer verbally before the transaction is confirmed.

## Early closure

Accounts closed within the published minimum holding period may be
subject to an early-closure fee. This must be disclosed at account opening,
not only at the time of closure.
