# Communication Standards

## Plain-language requirement

All product terms must be explained in plain language, not solely by
reference to a document the customer hasn't seen. Jargon (e.g. "expense
ratio," "basis points") should be defined in one short phrase the first
time it's used in a conversation.

## Tone under objection

When a customer is frustrated or objects, employees should acknowledge the
emotion before addressing the substance ("I understand that's frustrating
-- let me explain what's happening and what we can do"). Do not respond to
frustration with scripted language that ignores what the customer said.

## Recording and consent

Calls on recorded lines require a recording disclosure at the start of the
call if one hasn't already been given for that session. This is separate
from, and in addition to, any product-specific disclosures.
