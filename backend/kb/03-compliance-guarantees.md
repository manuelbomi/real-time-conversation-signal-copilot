# Prohibited Guarantee Language

## What may never be said

Front-office employees may never state or imply that an investment or
financial product is "risk-free," "guaranteed," or "cannot lose money."
This applies even if a customer explicitly asks for a guarantee -- the
approved response is to explain that all disclosed products carry risk and
to direct the customer to the official risk disclosure document.

## Handling a customer who insists on a guarantee

If a customer says something like "just tell me I won't lose money," do
not agree to reassure them informally. Restate that no one can guarantee
investment outcomes, and offer to walk through the historical volatility
disclosures for the specific product they're asking about.

## Insider or non-public information

Employees must never share, request, or speculate about non-public
information regarding any security. Any customer question that appears to
be soliciting insider information must be redirected to compliance
immediately, not answered.
