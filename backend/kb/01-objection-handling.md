# Objection Handling

## Price concerns

When a customer says the price or fee is too high, acknowledge the concern
before responding with value. Do not offer an unapproved discount over the
phone. Approved response pattern: restate the concern, confirm what is
included at the current tier, and offer to connect them with a specialist
if they want to discuss tier options. Never claim a competitor's pricing
without a verified, current comparison sheet.

## Hesitation about timing

If a customer says they want to "think about it," offer to send a written
summary of what was discussed and schedule a specific follow-up time. Do
not use high-pressure language ("this offer expires today") unless a time-
limited promotion is active and disclosed in the approved promotions list.

## Trust and track record concerns

If a customer questions the company's track record, refer only to publicly
published performance history and disclosures. Do not state or imply
specific future returns or outcomes.
