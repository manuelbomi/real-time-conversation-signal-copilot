# Recognizing and Responding to Buying Signals

## Readiness language

Phrases like "how do I get started," "what's the next step," or "let's do
this" indicate readiness to proceed. The approved next step is to confirm
eligibility requirements verbally, then transfer to account opening or
schedule a specialist call -- do not attempt to close an account opening
verbally without the required identity verification step.

## Confirming before advancing

Before advancing a customer who shows a buying signal, confirm they
understand the product's key terms (fees, any lock-in period, cancellation
policy) in plain language. This protects the customer and creates a record
that terms were disclosed.

## Avoiding false urgency

Do not manufacture urgency ("only a few spots left") to convert a buying
signal into a commitment unless that scarcity is genuinely true and
verifiable from current inventory/capacity systems.
