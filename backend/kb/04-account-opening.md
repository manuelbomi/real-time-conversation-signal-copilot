# Account Opening Process

## Identity verification

Every new account requires identity verification (government-issued ID
number and address confirmation) before any funds can be accepted. This
step cannot be skipped or deferred, even for an existing customer opening
an additional account.

## Required disclosures

Before an account is opened, the customer must be read (or shown, if on a
video/chat channel) the fee schedule and the cancellation/closure policy.
Confirmation that these were disclosed must be logged in the account-
opening record.

## Minimum funding and holds

New accounts are subject to a standard funds-availability hold on initial
deposits. Employees should set the expectation that the account may not
be immediately fully funded and tradable.
