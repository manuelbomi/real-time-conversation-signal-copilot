# Escalation Paths

## When to escalate to a specialist

Escalate to a licensed specialist when a customer asks for advice that
requires a license the front-office employee does not hold (e.g. specific
investment recommendations, tax advice, or estate planning questions).
Front-office employees may share published general information but may not
give personalized advice in these areas.

## When to escalate to compliance directly

Escalate directly to compliance, not a specialist, for: any request that
resembles solicitation of non-public information, any customer statement
that could indicate financial elder abuse or coercion, and any request to
backdate or alter a transaction record.

## Escalation SLA

Specialist escalations should be acknowledged to the customer with an
expected callback window (same business day for standard requests). The
customer should never be left without a concrete next step and timeframe.
